Car accident questions

What does it cost to hire Hope Law Firm?

Nothing up front. Consultations are always free, and there is no fee until we win your case.

How long do I have to file a car accident claim in Iowa?

Iowa has a two-year statute of limitations for personal injury lawsuits. Evidence and witness memories fade well before that, so the sooner you call, the better.

What if we were both at fault?

You may still have a claim. Under Iowa law, you can recover damages as long as you weren't more than 50% at fault, though your share of fault reduces the amount. An attorney can look at the facts with you.

I was hurt in someone else's car. Who pays?

The owner's insurance company typically covers the claim if you were a passenger or driving another person's car. You may also be able to file with your own auto insurer.

I already have a lawyer. Can I switch?

Yes. You have the right to change attorneys. Choose "thinking about switching" in the questions above and the team will talk it through with you.
